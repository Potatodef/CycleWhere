Singapore group route planner

Find a cycling route that still leaves the ride home feeling fair.

One meetup point, each rider's nearest MRT or LRT station, and route endings compared by how balanced the trip home feels for the whole group.

2 to 10 riders MRT-first inputs Mobile-friendly cards

The idea

A route can be great and still be unfair.

Most planners optimise the ride itself. CycleWhere explored a second question: after the group ride ends, how difficult is it for each person to get home?

Riders choose a shared start and the train station they live near. The interface compares plausible cycling routes, then surfaces endings that keep the longest and shortest onward journeys reasonably close.
